# Test Plan: REQ-002 - Optional two-party approval on any step

## Testing Objectives

**Requirement:** Optional two-party approval on any step
**Category:** functional
**Description:** Any step in any lifecycle phase can be marked as requiring two-party approval by policy. When a step requires approval, execution halts at that step in status 'awaiting_approval' until a second, distinct human identity approves it. The requester may never approve their own request (enforced server-side against the IAP-verified identity, not a client-supplied field). Approval policy is configuration, not code: a policy document declares, per phase and per step, whether approval is required, which operator role may approve, and an optional expiry after which the pending approval auto-rejects. Approvals and rejections are recorded with the approver identity, timestamp, and free-text justification. Rejecting a step terminates the request in status 'rejected' and dispatches no further steps.

## Acceptance Criteria

Criterion text lives HERE once; every section below cites the AC id.

- **AC-REQ-002-1** [automated] [VERIFIED] A step whose policy sets requiresApproval=true halts in status 'awaiting_approval' and dispatches no Workspace call until approved
- **AC-REQ-002-2** [automated] [VERIFIED] An approval attempt by the same identity that created the request is rejected with 403 and the step remains in 'awaiting_approval'
- **AC-REQ-002-3** [automated] [VERIFIED] An approval by a distinct identity holding the required approver role transitions the step to 'ready' and dispatches its execution task
- **AC-REQ-002-4** [automated] [VERIFIED] A rejection transitions the request to 'rejected', records approver identity, timestamp and justification, and dispatches no further steps
- **AC-REQ-002-5** [automated] [VERIFIED] An approval or rejection submitted with an empty or whitespace-only justification is refused by the server with 400, independently of any client-side check
- **AC-REQ-002-6** [automated] [VERIFIED] Approval policy is read from configuration at request-creation time and snapshotted onto the request, so a later policy edit cannot retroactively change an in-flight request's approval requirements
- **AC-REQ-002-7** [automated] [PENDING] When a step enters 'awaiting_approval' with an expiry configured, a Cloud Task is scheduled for the expiry instant; if the approval is still pending when that task fires the request terminates in 'rejected' with reason 'approval_expired', and if the step was already decided the task is a no-op
- **AC-REQ-002-8** [automated] [PENDING] With requiresApproval=false for every step, a request runs end to end with no human interaction beyond submission
